ACNE04 was first released as two separate archives: one with the images and their severity grades, the other with the lesion boxes. Using both meant downloading each, matching files by name, and joining them by hand.

Copies on Hugging Face did not solve this. Some held as few as 394 of the 1,457 images, some dropped the lesion boxes, and some reused the labels for unrelated tasks with no severity grade left. A model tested on one of these copies cannot be compared with the original benchmark.
