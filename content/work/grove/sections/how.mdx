Grove runs [Qwen2.5 0.5B Instruct](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct), a small open language model from Alibaba Cloud, through [WebLLM](https://github.com/mlc-ai/web-llm). WebLLM uses the device's graphics chip through WebGPU, so the model thinks on the machine in front of you.

- **Getting ready.** The model downloads once, a few hundred megabytes, while the person reads the welcome screen. The browser keeps it, so the next visit opens straight into the chat.
- **Answering.** Each reply is written one piece at a time and shown as it is made. A short instruction tells the model to use plain English, explain any jargon, and gently point to a licensed professional for medical, legal or money decisions.
- **Offline.** After the first load Grove works with no connection. If the connection drops, a line under the box says so and the chat keeps going.
- **Remembering.** Past chats are saved in the browser's own storage, so the person can come back to them or delete them.
