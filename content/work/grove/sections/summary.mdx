Grove is a free assistant you can ask anything: write an email, explain a bill, plan a meal, work out your rights. You open it, it gets ready once, and from then on it answers on your own phone or computer. There is no account to make, and nothing you type is sent to anyone.

It is built for people who do not always have easy access to expert help, and who should not have to hand over their questions to get an answer. The screens are deliberately simple: one headline, one button, then a chat.
