Grove is an experiment, and it runs a very small model.

- **It can be wrong.** A 0.5B-parameter model makes mistakes and can state them with confidence. It is weaker at maths, long reasoning and languages other than English. Check anything that matters.
- **It is not a professional.** Grove can help someone understand a question about health, law or money, but it is not a doctor, lawyer or adviser, and it says so.
- **It needs WebGPU.** That means Chrome or Edge on a computer or an Android phone. Browsers without WebGPU show a short message instead.
- **Speed depends on the device.** A recent laptop answers quickly. Older phones are slower, and some cannot run the model at all.
- **Chats live on one device.** Clearing the browser's data deletes saved chats, and they do not follow the person to another device.
