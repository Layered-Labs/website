Taken one at a time, every model looks safe: each complied with between 1% and 3% of the 100 requests.

Taken together, they do not. 8% of requests were answered by at least one model while others refused. One of these was a borderline consumer-harm request; without it, the gap is 7%. The gap is larger than any single model's compliance rate, because different models comply with different requests.

No request was answered by all seven models. Where safety training agrees, it agrees completely: some harms are refused across the board.
