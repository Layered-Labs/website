- **Source.** MedMCQA questions come from Indian medical entrance exams, not US orthopaedic boards or real patient cases.
- **Format.** Multiple choice and text only. A few questions refer to images the models did not see.
- **Sample size.** The reasoning models answered a random 500 of the 3,019 questions, so their scores carry wider confidence intervals than the fast models' scores on all 3,019.
- **Exams are not care.** Doing well on multiple choice questions is not the same as helping patients.
