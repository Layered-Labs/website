BenchBase converts medical multiple-choice benchmarks into one format and scores every model on every benchmark the same way, with one command. It covers five benchmarks and 200,784 questions: MedQA, MedMCQA, PubMedQA, the medical subjects of MMLU, and TRIAGE. It is for researchers who need numbers they can compare across models, papers and time, and it is open source under the MIT license.
