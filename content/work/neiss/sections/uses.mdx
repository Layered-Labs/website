For research on injury trends, product safety, sports and recreation injuries, and emergency department use, and for policy analysis and journalism.

Each row is one sampled visit, not one injury nationwide. National estimates come from summing the `Weight` column, never from counting rows:

```python
import pandas as pd
df = pd.read_parquet("hf://datasets/Layered-Labs/neiss-injury-data/neiss_all.parquet")
picks = df[(df["Product_1"] == 3235) & (df["year"] >= 2015)]  # pickleball
print(f"{picks['Weight'].sum():,.0f} estimated ED visits, 2015 to 2024")
```

It covers emergency visits only: injuries treated elsewhere, and deaths without an emergency visit, are not in it. Race and ethnicity coding changed over the years, so compare those fields across years with care.
