- **English only.** The voice model is trained on English speech.
- **Speed depends on the device.** Speech takes a few seconds to make on a typical laptop and longer on older phones. Browsers that cannot run the model on several threads, such as Safari today, are slower.
- **Quality depends on the recording.** A quiet room and a clear ten seconds give the closest voice. Background music or other speakers reduce it.
- **One voice at a time.** People can save several voices but speak with one.
- **Consent.** Only clone a voice you have permission to use. The model is released under CC BY 4.0.

Wellspring is an experiment, and it will change as people use it.
