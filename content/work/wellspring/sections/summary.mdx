Wellspring speaks anything you type in your own voice. You record about ten seconds of yourself reading a sentence, or upload a video or audio file you already have, and from then on every message you send is spoken aloud as you. It is free, needs no account, and runs entirely in the browser on a phone or computer.

It is built for people who type more easily than they speak, including older adults and anyone whose voice is tiring, changing or at risk. The screens are deliberately simple: large text, one action per screen, and a chat where each message is spoken as soon as it is sent.
