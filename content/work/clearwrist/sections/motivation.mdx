GRAZPEDWRI-DX is one of the largest public sets of children's wrist X-rays, but it ships as four zip archives (16 GB), a separate table of labels and a separate folder of boxes, joined by file name.

Copies on Hugging Face stripped the labels, leaving images with no fracture label, no patient ID and no boxes, and no way to build a fair test split. A model can then be tested on X-rays of a child it has already seen in training, which makes it look better than it is.
