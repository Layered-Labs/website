One row per ZIP code: borough and neighborhood, household internet access, the number of broadband providers and their top speeds, fiber availability, the electricity provider and its rates, the monthly power cost of three kinds of AI machine, and grid reliability.

Power is not the barrier. All 311 ZIP codes are served by the same utility, Con Edison, with commercial power at 17.82 cents per kWh and about 15 minutes of outage per customer a year, excluding major events. Running a Mac mini around the clock costs about $4.56 a month in electricity.
