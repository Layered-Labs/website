- **Coverage.** 181 of the 311 ZIP codes have household broadband figures; the rest, most of them in Manhattan, have no household figures in the census table. 197 have FCC provider data.
- **Speeds.** The FCC lists some carrier-grade fiber at over a terabit per second, which no clinic can buy. A second speed column caps the figure at 10 Gbps, the fastest tier a clinic can realistically get.
- **Power cost** is computed from each machine's typical draw and the commercial rate, running all day, every day.
