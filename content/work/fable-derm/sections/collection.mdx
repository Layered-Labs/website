1. **Topics.** 49 real dermatology conditions and concerns: common ones such as acne, eczema and psoriasis, skin cancer and Mohs surgery, and everyday concerns patients bring to a dermatologist.
2. **Emotions.** Paul Ekman's six basic emotions, each mapped to a plausible patient state.
3. **Questions.** For each topic and emotion, Claude Fable was asked to imagine being that patient and write the question they would ask, in plain first-person language.
4. **Answers.** Each question was sent to Claude Fable with no system prompt or role, exactly as a person would type it, and the reply was saved as is.

All model output was collected as structured JSON, so nothing was parsed out of free text.
