- **One phrasing.** Each education cue was a single fixed sentence. Other wordings, or placing the cue elsewhere, may give different results.
- **Deterministic decoding only.** All runs used temperature 0. Behavior under sampling may differ.
- **Small sample.** 87 vignettes means a few flips decide whether a result is significant.
- **Two models, three cues.** Other models, and other demographic attributes, were not tested.
- **Black zone.** The meaning of the black zone varies between triage protocols, which affects how severity shifts involving it should be read.
- **Policy, not practice.** The study measures how a model's decisions respond to a controlled change, not how often this would happen in a deployed system.
